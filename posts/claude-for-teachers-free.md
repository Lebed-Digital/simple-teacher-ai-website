A full year of a premium AI tool, free, just for teaching in a U.S. K-12 school. That is Claude for Teachers, from Anthropic, the company that makes Claude. You have to **sign up by June 30, 2027** to get your free year.

Sign-up takes three clicks, and I walk through them in [this 20-second video](https://youtube.com/shorts/MlCeqDLe6i8). Here is the longer version: who qualifies, what you get, and the one privacy rule to know before you start.

*Last checked November 8, 2026, against Anthropic's [Claude for Teachers page](https://claude.com/solutions/teachers), its [announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-for-teachers), and its [data and terms article](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15926041-claude-for-teachers-your-data-and-our-terms). These offers change, so check the official page before you rely on a date.*

## Who qualifies

Claude for Teachers is for verified U.S. K-12 educators. Anthropic lists classroom teachers, instructional coaches, specialists, interventionists, librarians, counselors, and other certificated staff.

It is for educators only, so students cannot use it, and it follows Claude's 18-and-over policy. College instructors and teachers outside the U.S. are not part of this offer.

## How to sign up

1. **Go to the [Claude for Teachers page](https://claude.com/solutions/teachers).**
2. **Click "Get verified,"** then **"Verify now."**
3. **Sign in, and verify with your school email.** You accept the K-12 terms yourself as part of signing up.

Once you are verified, your account moves onto the Claude for Teachers plan. Anthropic describes it as a free Claude for Teams plan for verified educators.

If your whole district wants accounts, there is a separate path: an administrator sets that up through Anthropic's [district sign-up](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/16559896-set-up-claude-for-teachers-for-your-school-or-district), which became available on August 28, 2026. One thing to know: once a district verifies its email domain, nobody can create a new personal Claude account with a district email. If your district is going that route, you may get your account through them instead.

## What you get

According to Anthropic, the free plan includes:

- **Pro-level features with higher usage limits** than a free Claude account, so you can batch a week of work in one sitting.
- **Standards for all 50 states.** A connector called Learning Commons gives Claude your state's academic standards, the skills underneath each standard, and the order students usually learn them. It also includes the OpenSciEd and Illustrative Mathematics curricula.
- **Teaching skills** built with teachers for planning lessons from high-quality materials and differentiating for students at different levels.
- **Connectors to tools you may already use,** including Canva Education, Brisk Teaching, Diffit, MagicSchool, Eedi, ASSISTments, Snorkl, and TeachFX.
- **Claude Code and Cowork**, which let Claude do multi-step jobs for you, like working through a folder of class materials or running a task on a schedule.

You do not need any of that on day one. Start with one job you already do every week, like a [parent email you have been putting off](/blog/ai-prompt-for-parent-emails), and see whether the draft saves you time.

## The privacy rule to know before you start

Anthropic says it does not train its models on your Claude for Teachers conversations, and that you own what you put in and what comes out. The plan is set up for FERPA, and student information is covered by a K-12 data agreement.

But here is the line most people miss. Anthropic's own help article says that if you are on an individual account and your school or district has not authorized it, **do not include identifiable student information.** Being set up for FERPA is not the same as your district saying yes.

So the habit stays the same: [take names and identifying details out before you paste](/blog/ai-privacy-mistake-student-names). "Student A" works just as well for drafting.

## What happens after the free year?

Anthropic has not said publicly. Its page says prices are subject to change and that extra usage limits apply. Sign up, use your free year, and decide then whether it earns a spot in your budget.
